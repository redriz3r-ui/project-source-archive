2 · Identify the object

Put a distinctive colored marker on a non-contact part of the object, such as its base. Never place a marker on a surface intended for oral or bodily contact.

3 · Tune recognition

Higher sensitivity needs fewer consistent frames. Start conservatively and watch for false positives.

Move the camera or object until the mouth and marker remain visible across the intended motion. Try brief passes and occlusions; the state should avoid false active detections.
