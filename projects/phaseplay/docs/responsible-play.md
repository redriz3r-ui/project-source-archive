# Responsible-play boundaries

PhasePlay is an entertainment budgeting tool, not gambling, financial, or addiction-treatment advice. Product copy and automated responses must never imply that previous spins affect future outcomes, that a game is due, or that changing wagers recovers losses.

The app should always allow the player to end a session, surface the configured stop floor, and keep protected cash outside the playable balance. Future production work should add age/jurisdiction controls, self-exclusion resources, cool-off controls, privacy review, and legal/app-store review before launch.
